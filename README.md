# Panduan Rumah · 家务手册

A household manual for domestic helpers in Indonesia, written mainly in Bahasa Indonesia with Chinese alongside. It is built for Chinese families who have to teach the same house rules again every time they hire a new helper.

**Live:** https://denniszhou2014-cmyk.github.io/panduan-rumah/

## What it does

- **Lessons:** page-by-page lessons the helper swipes through. Each page has one illustration and one short sentence, read aloud in Indonesian. Each topic ends with a picture quiz.
- **Two languages:** switch between Indonesian with Chinese alongside, or Indonesian only.
- **Works like an app:** add it to the phone home screen. It keeps working offline after the first visit.

## Topics

1. Cleaning cloths by colour: which colour cleans which place
2. Knives and cutting boards: fruit, raw meat, vegetables and cooked food kept apart
3. Washing and drying: where to dry clothes in sun or rain
4. Cleaning schedule: daily, weekly and monthly tasks for each room
5. Daily routine for the helper, from 06:00 to 19:30
6. Shopping and money: grocery orders on Segari reimbursed by bank transfer, a fixed cash float checked every Sunday, and an approval limit per order

## Files

- `index.html` is the whole app (content, illustrations, logic)
- `sw.js` is the offline cache
- `manifest.webmanifest` and `icon-*.png` provide the home-screen icon
